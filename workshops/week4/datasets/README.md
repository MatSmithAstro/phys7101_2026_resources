# Week 4 datasets

**`cavendish_1798.csv`**: Henry Cavendish's 29 measurements of the mean density of the Earth (g/cm³), in the order he made them, as printed in his table.
Source: H. Cavendish (1798), "Experiments to determine the density of the Earth", *Phil. Trans. R. Soc.* **88**, 469–526, via S. M. Stigler (1977), "Do robust estimators deal with real data?", *Ann. Statist.* **5**, 1055–1098 (Table 8), as distributed in the `HistData` R package and Rdatasets. Cavendish changed the suspension wire after the sixth measurement. The third value (4.88) is as printed; Stigler notes that Cavendish's own arithmetic suggests it should have been 5.88.

**`michelson_1879.csv`**: Albert Michelson's 100 measurements of the speed of light in air (km/s) from June–July 1879: five experiments of 20 runs each.
Source: A. A. Michelson (1880), "Experimental determination of the velocity of light made at the U.S. Naval Academy, Annapolis", *Astronomical Papers* **1**, 135–178, via Stigler (1977), Table 6, as distributed in the `MASS` R package (`michelson`) and Rdatasets.

Both are historical measurements in the public domain.
