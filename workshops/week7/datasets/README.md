# Week 7 datasets

**`sunflower_growth.csv`**: the height of a sunflower (*Helianthus annuus*), in cm, measured every 7 days from planting (`day` 0) to day 84. No measurement uncertainties are given.
Source: H. S. Reed and R. H. Holland (1919), "The growth rate of an annual plant *Helianthus*", *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* **5**, 135–144, as distributed by the Quantitative Environmental Learning Project (Seattle Central College). Historical measurements in the public domain.
